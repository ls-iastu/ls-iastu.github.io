I am Shuo Liu, a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Physics at Princeton University. Before joining Princeton, I received my Ph.D. from Tsinghua University in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Hong Yao, and my B.S. from Beijing Normal University in 2020.

I am interested in nonequilibrium dynamics, quantum information and computing, strongly correlated systems, and AI for physics, including topics such as time crystals, the quantum Mpemba effect, and entanglement transitions.
